About me

A safe, supportive space to be heard.

Taking the first step towards seeking support can feel overwhelming, but it can also be the beginning of meaningful change. Whether you're navigating stress, anxiety, relationship challenges, life transitions, or simply seeking personal growth, I'm here to provide a warm, confidential space where you can feel heard, understood, and supported.

Sessions are held securely online, so you can access counselling from anywhere in South Africa. Together, we'll work towards building resilience, gaining insight, and developing the tools to help you move forward with greater confidence, balance, and wellbeing.

I'm Emma Rossouw, a Registered Specialist Wellness Counsellor (ASCHP) based in Cape Town, passionate about creating a space where people feel heard, understood, and empowered to navigate life's challenges.
