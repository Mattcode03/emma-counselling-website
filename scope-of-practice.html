Scope of Practice

What You Can Expect

As a Specialist Wellness Counsellor, I hold the highest level of registration within the Association for Supportive Counsellors and Holistic Practitioners (ASCHP).

My Role

Professional, Non-Diagnostic Support

My role is to provide professional, non-diagnostic counselling that promotes emotional wellbeing, resilience, and personal growth. I support individuals in managing everyday emotional challenges, stress, relationship difficulties, life transitions, and other concerns that impact overall wellbeing.

Referrals

Beyond My Scope

As an ASCHP-registered Specialist Wellness Counsellor, I do not diagnose mental health disorders or prescribe medication. Should your needs fall outside my scope of practice, I will gladly refer you to a trusted HPCSA-registered mental health professional, such as a Registered Counsellor, Psychologist, Psychiatrist, or another appropriate healthcare practitioner, ensuring you receive the level of care best suited to your needs.

A safe, tailored, confidential space

  • Every person's journey is different, which is why I tailor each session to your unique needs, goals, and experiences.
  • My aim is to create a safe, supportive, confidential, and non-judgemental space where you feel comfortable to talk openly and be yourself.
  • Sessions are held online, so you can access counselling from the comfort and privacy of your own home or any space where you feel safe and relaxed.
  • I use a range of evidence-based counselling approaches, including Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), Solution-Focused Therapy, and Narrative Therapy, depending on what will best support you.
  • Together, we'll explore the challenges you're facing, gain a better understanding of your experiences, and work towards meaningful, lasting change.
  • My goal is to equip you with practical tools, coping strategies, and greater self-awareness so you feel more confident and resilient long after our sessions have ended.
