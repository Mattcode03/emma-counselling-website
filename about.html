About me

Hello, I'm Emma Rossouw.

Registered with
ASCHP

Association for Supportive Counsellors and Holistic Practitioners — Specialist Wellness Counsellor.

Taking the first step towards seeking support can feel overwhelming, but it can also be the beginning of meaningful change.

Whether you're navigating stress, anxiety, relationship challenges, life transitions, or simply seeking personal growth, I'm here to provide a warm, confidential space where you can feel heard, understood, and supported.

Sessions are held securely online, so you can access counselling from anywhere in South Africa. Together, we'll work towards building resilience, gaining insight, and developing the tools to help you move forward with greater confidence, balance, and wellbeing.

I'm Emma Rossouw, a Registered Specialist Wellness Counsellor (ASCHP) based in Cape Town, passionate about creating a safe, supportive space where people feel heard, understood, and empowered to navigate life's challenges.

I take an integrative approach to counselling, recognising that every individual is unique and that there is no one-size-fits-all approach to therapy. Together, we will tailor our sessions to your specific needs, goals, and circumstances.

Drawing on evidence-based approaches such as Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), Solution-Focused Therapy, and Narrative Therapy, I work collaboratively with you within a person-centred framework. This means you'll be met with empathy, respect, and acceptance while we explore practical strategies and meaningful insights to help you navigate life's challenges, build resilience, and move towards the life you want to create.

“Every person's journey is different, which is why I tailor each session to your unique needs, goals, and experiences.”

How I work

Three approaches I draw on

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy

Noticing the patterns in how you think, feel and act — and finding practical ways to shift the ones that no longer serve you.

Solution-Focused Therapy

Starting from what already works in your life and building on it, with clear goals and steps you can actually take.

Narrative Therapy

Looking at the story you hold about yourself and your experiences, and finding room to retell it on your own terms.
