Services

Online Counselling Services

I offer secure online counselling sessions to clients across South Africa, allowing you to access professional support from the comfort and privacy of your own space. Whether you're in Cape Town or anywhere else in the country, virtual therapy provides a flexible and convenient way to prioritise your mental wellbeing.

Individual Online Counselling

For children, adolescents, and adults — tailored to your specific needs, goals, and circumstances.

Couples Online Counselling

A collaborative, person-centred space to work through relationship, marriage, and family challenges together.
